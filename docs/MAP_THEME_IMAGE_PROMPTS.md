# Kembara Pintar map theme prompts

Use these prompts to generate a wide background illustration for the student progress map. Generate one image per theme with the same composition so the theme can be swapped without changing checkpoint positions.

## Shared art direction

Create a cheerful children's educational board-game map in a bright 3D storybook style, viewed from a slightly elevated top-down angle. Use a wide landscape composition with a winding cream-coloured stepping-stone path that travels from the lower-left start to the upper-right finish. Leave clear open spaces at five fixed checkpoint positions along the path: lower-left, lower-middle, centre, upper-middle, and upper-right. Include small landmarks around the route, with no words, letters, numbers, logos, UI, buttons, or readable signs. Keep the centre of each checkpoint area visually simple so a circular progress marker can be placed on top later. Use high contrast, clean silhouettes, warm daylight, soft shadows, playful but uncluttered details, and a palette suitable for children aged 6-10.

## Hutan Mistik

Create the shared art direction as a magical Malaysian rainforest adventure: giant tropical leaves, tree roots, a glowing river, a wooden bridge, a friendly hornbill, fireflies, a small waterfall, mossy stones, and a misty emerald canopy. Use jewel-green foliage, turquoise water, warm sunlight, and small coral and yellow accents. The mood is mysterious but safe and welcoming, never dark or frightening.

## Pulau Lanun Ceria

Create the shared art direction as a sunny tropical island treasure trail: turquoise sea, sandy coves, palm trees, a wooden pier, a friendly lighthouse, a tiny sailboat, treasure chests, seashells, and a cheerful parrot. Use aqua, ocean blue, mango yellow, coral red, and leafy green. Make the island feel adventurous, open, and friendly rather than dangerous.

## Angkasa Bintang

Create the shared art direction as a colourful space journey: a small launch pad, friendly planets, floating asteroids, a moon base, a bright rocket, star constellations, and a ringed planet. Use deep navy only in the sky, with cyan, violet, lemon yellow, coral, and white highlights. Keep the route readable and make the scene playful and educational rather than futuristic or intimidating.

## Kota Robot

Create the shared art direction as a bright child-friendly learning city: a school, library, science lab, park, pedestrian bridges, small electric buses, trees, fountains, and friendly helper robots. Use sky blue, mint green, sunshine yellow, coral, and clean white buildings. Keep streets broad and simple, with a clear winding route and no text on buildings or signs.

## Lembah Dinosaur

Create the shared art direction as a friendly prehistoric valley: fern forests, rounded mountains, a shallow river, a volcano far in the background, dinosaur footprints, a fossil dig site, and three gentle smiling dinosaurs. Use leafy green, sky blue, warm orange, cream, and red accents. The dinosaurs must look curious and harmless, with no sharp teeth or scary expressions.

## Negative prompt

No text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no logo, no interface, no map labels, no dark horror mood, no realistic violence, no weapons, no scary monsters, no tightly cropped objects, no empty blank background, no extreme perspective, no checkpoint icons baked into the image, no path branching into confusing dead ends.
